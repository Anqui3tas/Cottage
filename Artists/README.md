# Artists

All artwork here belongs to its artists. The repository's MIT license does not apply to it. Each artist's `.txt` profile lists what you can and can't do with their work. Being publicly visible doesn't make it free to reuse.

The app is free for everyone, and artists never pay to take part. Valid DMCA takedown notices will be honored; open an issue to raise a concern about any artwork.

Only original, artist-made work is accepted. AI-generated artwork is not.

## Submitting

Start from `Templates/Artist-Submission`. Your profile needs a display name, links, a description, and usage terms. A banner image is optional.

```text
Artists/ArtistName/
  ArtistName.txt
  Artist image.png
  Collection/
    01.Pack/
      01.Image.png
```

Number folders and images to set their order; the numbers are hidden in the app. Images must be PNG, up to 20 MB and 8192 pixels per side. The app makes smaller copies for Messages and leaves your originals untouched.
